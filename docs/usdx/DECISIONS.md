# USDX Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) & Assumption Register

## 1. Context & Scope

This document records the confirmed Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) established for the USDX stablecoin protocol, alongside an explicit register of items deferred to subsequent phases.

---

## 2. Confirmed Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

### ADR-01: Smart Contract Toolchain Selection
- **Status:** Approved
- **Decision:** Select **Foundry** (`forge`, `cast`) as the primary smart-contract compilation and testing framework.
- **Rationale:** Foundry provides fast compilation, native Solidity testing, gas profiling, and EVM target configuration capabilities.

### ADR-02: EVM Target & Compiler Compatibility Baseline (`london` EVM & `0.8.20` solc)
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Modern Solidity compilers (`solc >= 0.8.20`) default to the `shanghai` EVM version, which uses the `PUSH0` instruction. The TRON Virtual Machine (TVM) does not currently support `PUSH0`.
- **Decision:** Adopt Solidity **`0.8.20`** as the current compatibility baseline and **`london`** as the current conservative compilation baseline in `foundry.toml`.
- **Rationale:** Prevents generated bytecode from containing unsupported opcodes (`PUSH0`) that would cause smart contract deployment failure on TRON networks.
- **Consequences:** Developers and CI pipelines must strictly respect the pinned compiler version (`0.8.20`) and EVM target (`london`).

### ADR-03: Token Standard, Decimals & Initial Supply
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Stablecoins operating on TRON must conform to standard wallet and exchange expectations while maintaining accounting compatibility with major fiat stablecoins (e.g., USDT, USDC).
- **Decision:** Adopt a **TRC-20-compatible** token design with **6 decimal places** and an **initial supply of zero**.
- **Rationale:** 6 decimals matches TRON USDT/USDC conventions and prevents rounding discrepancies during USD fiat conversions (1 cent = 10,000 units). Initial supply of zero ensures tokens are only created upon verified fiat reserve deposits.
- **Consequences:** Off-chain systems must format amounts using 6 decimal place precision (`1 USD = 1,000,000` base units). No unbacked pre-minted tokens exist at initial deployment.

### ADR-04: Hard On-Chain Solvency Invariant
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Minting tokens without confirmed reserves creates unbacked circulating supply and insolvency risk.
- **Decision:** The USDX core contract must enforce `totalSupply <= eligibleReserves` on every mint call (specifically `totalSupply + amount <= eligibleReserves`).
- **Rationale:** Guarantees that even if operational minter keys or off-chain backend systems are compromised, token supply cannot exceed verified on-chain reserve attestations.
- **Consequences:** The reserve attestation oracle must maintain fresh data on-chain; stale reserve data fails closed.

### ADR-05: Multi-Attestor Quorum-Based Reserve Attestation
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Relying on a single attestor or off-chain feed for reserve verification introduces a single point of failure and vulnerability to compromised keys or rogue attestations.
- **Decision:** Adopt a **multi-attestor quorum-based reserve attestation** model on-chain, strictly enforcing that **no single attestor may unilaterally increase accepted eligible reserves**.
- **Rationale:** Prevents any single compromised oracle key or malicious attestor node from arbitrarily inflating reported reserves to permit unbacked minting.
- **Consequences:** Attestation contract must aggregate and validate signatures/reports from a configured threshold quorum of independent attesters before increasing `eligibleReserves`.

### ADR-06: UUPS Upgradeability, Governance Multisig & Timelock
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Smart contract upgradeability and administrative parameter management require secure, decentralized governance controls that protect users against unauthorized or abrupt contract modifications.
- **Decision:** Adopt **UUPS (Universal Upgradeable Proxy Standard)** upgradeability, controlled by a **3-of-5 multisig governance** mechanism coupled with a mandatory **timelock** for production privileged upgrades and configuration changes.
- **Rationale:** UUPS minimizes proxy gas overhead and keeps upgrade authorization logic within implementation contracts. A 3-of-5 multisig prevents single-key compromise, and the timelock ensures transparent advance notice for all administrative modifications.
- **Consequences:** Upgrade executions and privileged parameter modifications cannot occur atomically without passing the timelock delay.

### ADR-07: Mandatory Blacklist and Freeze Capabilities
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Legal, regulatory, and sanctions compliance (e.g., OFAC mandates, law enforcement orders) requires the ability to freeze assets associated with illicit activity.
- **Decision:** Incorporate **mandatory blacklist and account freeze capabilities** within the core USDX token contract logic.
- **Rationale:** Ensures compliance with international regulatory standards and law enforcement freeze orders, matching standard fiat-backed stablecoin risk management expectations.
- **Consequences:** Token transfer, minting, and burning functions must evaluate account blacklist status; frozen accounts are prevented from executing transactions.

### ADR-08: Fail-Closed Emergency Controls
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Operational disruptions, oracle failures, stale attestation data, or detected security anomalies require immediate mitigation to protect system solvency.
- **Decision:** Implement **fail-closed emergency controls** including an emergency pause mechanism and automatic mint halting on stale or invalid reserve data.
- **Rationale:** Ensuring system operations default to a paused/halted state during anomalous conditions prevents unauthorized minting or exploitative state transitions.
- **Consequences:** Emergency controllers can pause operations instantly, while unpausing requires governance intervention after risk resolution.

### ADR-09: Explicit Non-Atomic Redemption State Machine with Reconciliation & Recovery
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Fiat bank wire disbursements occur asynchronously off-chain, making end-to-end atomic on-chain redemption to bank account payout impossible.
- **Decision:** Implement an **explicit non-atomic redemption state machine** on-chain and off-chain, featuring formal reconciliation and recovery pathways for failed or delayed fiat wire transfers.
- **Rationale:** Properly models the real-world operational latency between on-chain token burning/escrow and off-chain banking settlement while ensuring deterministic state tracking and recovery mechanisms.
- **Consequences:** Redemptions progress through explicit states (Initiated -> Escrowed/Burned -> Disbursed / Reconciled / Refunded); edge cases such as rejected wire transfers are handled by formal recovery workflows.

### ADR-10: DEX Deployment Sequence Post-Mainnet Validation
- **Status:** Approved
- **Context:** Secondary market liquidity provision on decentralized exchanges (e.g., SunSwap) carries market volatility, liquidity bootstrapping, and operational risks during initial launch.
- **Decision:** Schedule **DEX deployment after controlled mainnet validation** of core contract infrastructure and reserve attestation systems, rather than treating DEX deployment as a prerequisite for initial mainnet launch.
- **Rationale:** Isolates core protocol stabilization, custodial bank integrations, and oracle feeds from secondary market trading dynamics during initial mainnet deployment.
- **Consequences:** Initial mainnet launch focuses exclusively on primary minting/redemptions with verified institutional partners before secondary market pools are established.
